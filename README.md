Sales Executive Performance Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that tracks the sales performance of 141 sales executives across 8 regions against their targets, using Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers and interactive controls.

Overview :-
A sales team needs a quick way to see who is performing well, who is falling behind, and how far everyone is from their target. This project turns a raw sales sheet into a one-page, interactive dashboard that answers those questions at a glance.

Business Questions Solved :-
Who are the top 5 sales executives by total sales? ,
Who are the bottom 5 sales executives, and need the most support? ,
Which executives have the highest target achievement (Target Hit %)? ,
Which executives are furthest away from their target? ,
How does performance change by region? (Region slicer)

Dashboard Features :-
Feature	Purpose
Pivot Tables	Summarise total sales, Target Hit % and Away From Target % per executive
Bar Chart	Top 5 executives by total sales ,
Pie Chart	Executives with the highest Target Hit % ,
Line Chart	Executives furthest away from target ,
Region Slicer	Filter the dashboard by region with one click ,
Checkbox controls	Interactive controls to switch dashboard sections on and off

Tools & Excel Concepts Used :-

1 Microsoft Excel
2 Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
3 Slicers
4 Form controls (checkboxes)
5 Calculated columns: Target Hit % = Total Sales ÷ Target, Away From Target % = 1 − Target Hit %
6 Top N filtering (Top 5 / Bottom 5)
7 Dashboard layout and formatting

Key Insights :- 
The team reached only 55% of its combined target. Total sales were 38,945 against a combined target of 70,500, an average of about 276 per executive versus a target of 500.
No executive hit the target. The best performer, Jagdish Chandra (Surat), reached 389 sales (77.8%), followed by Rachita Anupam (385), Miny Mole (384), Anikuttan (382) and Arvind Sharma (379).
A large group is struggling. 50 of 141 executives (about 35%) achieved less than half their target. The lowest performer, Omprakash O (Mumbai), made 143 sales, 71.4% away from target.
Regional gap: Nagpur has the best achievement rate (58.3%), while Ranchi is the lowest (50.5%), a gap of nearly 8 percentage points.
Daily pattern: Day 4 was the strongest day (8,323 sales) and Day 5 the weakest (7,352), suggesting sales tail off at the end of the period.

Recommendations :- 
Set up coaching and support for the bottom 5 executives and for Ranchi, Surat and Patna, which are below the team average.
Study what the top performers do differently and share those practices across the team.
Review whether a target of 500 in 5 days is realistic, since nobody reached it.
Investigate the Day 5 drop and consider incentives or end-of-period pushes.
